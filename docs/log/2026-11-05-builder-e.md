Note on `exporters.icsFor` in tz_test.dart: the golden records DTSTAMP as the frozen wall clock
read as UTC (the generator ran under TZ=UTC). DTSTAMP is printed from `now.toUtc()`, so under
TZ=Kolkata the local frozen clock would produce a different UTC instant and the case went RED in
all three zones - a test-model artefact, not a port bug (the JS `icsFor` default is `new Date()`,
an instant, and `toUTCString`/`toISOString` print it in UTC either way). The adapter now passes
that same instant explicitly (`now: DateTime.utc(w...)`), which is the only zone-independent way
to assert the printed string. Everything else in the case (DTSTART/DTEND/UID/SUMMARY) comes from
event dates and does not move. Documented at the top of tz_test.dart.
